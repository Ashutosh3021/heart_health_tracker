About HeartCheck AI

AI-powered heart disease risk assessment tool

What We Do

HeartCheck AI uses machine learning algorithms to analyze various health parameters and provide an assessment of heart disease risk. Our model is trained on clinical data and uses a Random Forest classifier to make predictions based on 13 key health indicators.

Key Features:

  • Instant risk assessment
  • User-friendly interface
  • Data visualization and analytics
  • Educational health information

How It Works

  1. Input: You provide 13 health parameters
  2. Processing: Data is standardized and analyzed
  3. Prediction: AI model calculates risk probability
  4. Result: You receive risk assessment with confidence level

Technical Details:

  • Random Forest Algorithm (100 trees)
  • Feature standardization with StandardScaler
  • Cross-validation for model reliability
  • Probability-based confidence scoring

Data Parameters Explained

Basic Information

  • Age: Age in years
  • Sex: Biological sex (0=Female, 1=Male)
  • Chest Pain: Type of chest pain experienced

Vital Signs

  • Blood Pressure: Resting BP (mmHg)
  • Cholesterol: Serum cholesterol (mg/dl)
  • Blood Sugar: Fasting glucose > 120 mg/dl

Heart Tests

  • ECG: Resting electrocardiogram
  • Max Heart Rate: Peak rate during exercise
  • Exercise Angina: Chest pain during exercise

Advanced Tests

  • ST Depression: Exercise-induced changes
  • ST Slope: Shape of ST segment
  • Vessels: Fluoroscopy vessel count
  • Thalassemia: Blood disorder test
